Waventra Vetric - Admin Review Management Update

Files included:
client/pages/admin/products.js
client/pages/admin/reviews.js
client/lib/api.js
server/routes/productRoutes.js

What changed:
1. Admin Products now has a "Reviews" link in each product row.
2. Clicking Reviews opens /admin/reviews?product=<productId>.
3. Admin Reviews page loads the selected product and its customer reviews.
4. Each review has Edit and Delete actions.
5. Edit opens a modal for rating (1-5) and comment (max 1000 chars).
6. Product rating and review count are recalculated by the existing backend controller.
7. Review update/delete routes are now protected by BOTH protect and adminOnly.
8. Customer review creation remains protected by login only.

Install:
Extract the ZIP over your project and replace the listed files.
Do not replace any .env files.
Restart both client and server after replacement.

Direct page:
http://localhost:3000/admin/reviews
